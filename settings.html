Security and your data

Security

Signed in

Your account is —, verified by Supabase — a real server-side login, not a check stored in this browser. Change your password below, or use "Forgot your password?" on the sign-in page to reset it by email.

Phone notifications

Off

Get a notification on this device every time a new booking is added, whether you add it on your phone or your laptop. Turn it on separately on each device you want notified.

Data

Everything on this dashboard — bookings and hosts — is stored in your account, synced across every device you sign into. Export a backup as extra insurance, or export to Excel to share your bookings with someone else. (Importing bookings from an Excel file is on the Bookings page now.)

Trash

Bookings you delete on the Bookings page stay here until you decide: restore one to put it back, or delete it forever. Nothing is removed automatically.

Start fresh

Give the dashboard a clean start from the month you pick. Older bookings are only hidden from the dashboard — nothing is deleted. They stay saved, still show on the Bookings and Hosts pages, and you can put them back on the dashboard any time.
